# Museum of the Year 3000 — The Feared Printer

A deadpan museum label from the year 3000 for the most relatable object of our era: the office printer. Future curators misread its notorious temperament as religion — it ignored polite requests, demanded panic as prayer, and answered with blank pages — a joke that is absurd, yet recognisably true to anyone who ever needed something printed in a hurry.
